    How to make your own Word template

    Mark each blank one of three ways. The site turns every marked blank into a field.

    • Highlight the text in yellow, for example a highlighted Firstname M. Lastname.
    • Type the blank in square brackets: [Subject].
    • Type the blank in double braces: {{Date}}.

    Blanks with the same text fill together, so [Name] in three places needs to be typed only once.

    Tongue and Quill reminders

    • Put the bottom line first. State the purpose or the action needed in the first sentence.
    • Use active voice and short sentences. Write "I request," not "It is requested."
    • Write dates as day, month and year: 23 November 2026.
    • Spell out an acronym the first time you use it, then use the acronym.
    • Use the correct grade abbreviation: C1C, Capt, Maj, Lt Col, MSgt.
    • Write signature block names in all capital letters: FIRSTNAME M. LASTNAME.
    • Proofread the finished form before you sign it.

    Check paperwork

    Upload a memorandum or a filled form. The site checks it against Tongue and Quill and lists corrections and suggestions. The file never leaves your device.

      What the checker looks for

      • Corrections: template placeholders, contractions, "memo," dates not written as day, month and year, and memorandum format errors such as paragraph numbering, the subject line and the signature block.
      • Suggestions: passive voice, long sentences, wordy phrases and acronyms that are not spelled out on first use.
      • The checker reads Word files and fillable PDFs. It cannot read scanned pages.
      • It catches common problems. It does not replace a careful proofread or your squadron's guidance.
